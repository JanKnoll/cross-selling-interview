Research Interview: Cross-Selling in the Off-Road Machinery Sector

Bachelor's thesis · Technische Hochschule Würzburg-Schweinfurt (THWS) · in cooperation with Schaeffler Technologies AG & Co. KG

About this study

Thank you for taking the time to participate. This conversation is part of a Bachelor's thesis conducted at THWS, supervised by Prof. Dr. Uwe Sponholz, in the context of a practical semester at Schaeffler Technologies AG & Co. KG. The study explores factors that support or hinder cross-selling within the off-road machinery sector.
