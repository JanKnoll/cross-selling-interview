Research Interview: Cross-Selling in the Offroad Sector

Bachelor's thesis · Technische Hochschule Würzburg-Schweinfurt (THWS) · in cooperation with Schaeffler Technologies AG & Co. KG

About this study

Thank you for taking the time to participate. This conversation is part of a Bachelor's thesis at THWS, supervised by Prof. Dr. Uwe Sponholz. The study examines which factors support or hinder cross-selling in the Offroad Sector and looks for ways to overcome existing barriers.

How this interview works

This is an AI-led interview: your responses are read and followed up on by an AI system rather than a human interviewer in real time, though all results are analyzed by the researcher as part of this academic thesis. There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your genuine experiences and perspectives. You can respond by typing or, if you prefer, by speaking your answer (your voice is automatically converted to text, which you can review before sending).

Interview period: 12–25 October 2026. The interview takes between 10 and 30 minutes. You can take a break at any time and continue via the same link until 25 October 2026. Your answers so far are saved. Please do not share your personal link.
